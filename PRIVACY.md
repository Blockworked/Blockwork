# Privacy Policy

The full privacy policy for Blockwork, its browser extension and the
Blockworked websites is at https://blockworked.com/privacy, and the terms of
service at https://blockworked.com/terms.

In short: Blockwork runs on your device. It has no accounts of its own,
telemetry, analytics or advertising, and it never sends your macros,
recordings, keystrokes or mouse activity anywhere. It goes online only for
the optional update check (GitHub download only), the community block
library, signing in to that library if you choose to, and blocks you add
that reach the network. The browser extension talks only to Blockwork on your
own computer.

Questions: support@blockworked.com.
